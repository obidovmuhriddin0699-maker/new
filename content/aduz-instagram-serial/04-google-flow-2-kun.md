# Google Flow uchun: 2-KUN — "Bir post — uch narx"

**Umumiy davomiylik:** ~39 s = 5 ta Flow klipi + 3 ta montaj bloki
**Format:** 9:16 · **Model:** Veo 3 (sinov — Fast, yakuniy — Quality)
**Yangi qahramon:** Dilnoza (videoning oxirida birinchi marta ko‘rinadi)

---

## 1-QADAM. Ingredients

1-kundagi **RUSTAM** va **DO‘KON** rasmlaridan qayta foydalaning, yangisini yaratmang. Shunda 1- va 2-qismdagi qahramon bir xil chiqadi.

Yangi ingredientlar:

**Ingredient 4 — DILNOZA**
```
Photorealistic full-body portrait of an Uzbek woman, 26 years old, slim, black hair tied in a low bun, thin black-framed glasses, smart and slightly ironic expression. Wearing an oversized beige knit sweater, black trousers, white wireless headphones resting around her neck, holding a smartphone. Standing, neutral pose, plain light grey studio background, soft even lighting, 9:16.
```

**Ingredient 5 — DILNOZANING XONASI**
```
Photorealistic cozy small home office in Uzbekistan: wooden desk with an open laptop, a ring light (switched off), a cup of green tea in a traditional blue-and-white Uzbek piala, a smartphone lying on the desk, wall covered with colorful sticky notes with no readable text, a potted green plant, window with soft daylight. No people. Vertical 9:16.
```

## 2-QADAM. Klip promptlari

> Dialog bo‘yicha qoida 1-kundagidek: o‘zbekcha replikani promptda qoldiring, talaffuz yomon chiqsa, ovozni montajda almashtiring.
> **Uchta adminning ovozi Flow'da yaratilmaydi.** Ularni montajda alohida yozilgan ovoz va chat pufakchalari bilan qo‘shasiz.

### KLIP 1 · 0–8 s · HOOK + UCHTA JAVOB
Ingredients: Rustam + Do‘kon
```
Vertical 9:16, photorealistic, cinematic. Eye-level medium shot, centered: the man from the reference stands in the furniture showroom from the reference and holds his smartphone up toward the camera like evidence, with an outraged comic expression, and says in Uzbek: "Bir xil post. Uch xil admin. Uch xil narx!" Then he turns the phone back to himself and reads three messages one after another, reacting each time: first raising his eyebrows in surprise, then squinting in confusion, then frowning suspiciously. Warm interior light. Static camera. Leave empty space at the top of the frame for graphics.
Audio: three soft message notification sounds, light comic music.
Avoid: readable text on the phone screen, logos, subtitles, extra people, change of clothing.
```
**Montajda shu klip ustiga (3–8 s):** ekran uchga bo‘linadi va har bir reaksiyaga bitta admin javobi to‘g‘ri keladi:
- 1-admin (tez gapiradigan erkak ovozi): *"300 ming. Bir soat tepada, keyin lentada."*
- 2-admin (rasmiy erkak ovozi): *"150 ming. 24 soat turadi, lekin faqat bitta rasm."*
- 3-admin (sokin ayol ovozi): *"Avval postni yuboring, keyin narxini aytaman."*
- Har narx yonida **"Shartli misol"** belgisi.

### KLIP 2 · 8–14 s · CHO‘T
Ingredients: Rustam + Do‘kon
```
Vertical 9:16, photorealistic. Close-up of the man's hands sliding beads on an old wooden abacus on the wooden cashier desk, then the camera tilts up to his confused face as he counts under his breath and says in Uzbek: "Bir soat tepada... yigirma to'rt soat... bitta rasm..." He pauses, looks up and exclaims: "Bu matematikami yo fizika?!" Warm light, shallow depth of field.
Audio: wooden abacus beads clicking.
Avoid: readable numbers or text, distorted fingers, extra hands.
```

### KLIP 3 · 14–20 s · SAVDOLASHISH
Ingredients: Rustam + Do‘kon
```
Vertical 9:16, photorealistic. Medium shot: the man holds the smartphone close to his mouth, recording a voice message, gesturing with his free hand like a confident bargainer at a bazaar, grinning, and says in Uzbek: "Ukam, 300 mingga ikkita qilib bering, bozor-ku!" Then a notification arrives; he reads the reply and his grin slowly fades into a deflated expression. Warm light. Handheld, subtle movement.
Audio: voice message recording sound, then one notification sound.
Avoid: readable text on screen, logos, extra people.
```
**Montajda (18–20 s):** javob pufakchasi va 1-admin ovozi: *"Aka, bu bozor emas, kanal."*

### MONTAJ BLOKI · 20–28 s · YECHIM (Flow'da yaratilmaydi)
- Grafika: uchta narx yonma-yon jadvalga tushadi. Ustunlar: **Narx · Turish muddati · Format · Ko‘rishlar** (*shartli misol*).
- Keyin 3 soniya @Targetreklammabot'ning haqiqiy ekran yozuvi: kanal kartochkasi (narx va ma’lumotlar).
- **Diktor:** "Narxni solishtirish uchun bir xil ma’lumot kerak: narx, muddat, format va ko‘rishlar. ADUZ'da kanallar ma’lumotini yonma-yon ko‘rib solishtirish mumkin."

### KLIP 4 · 28–31 s · PUNCHLINE
Ingredients: Rustam + Do‘kon
```
Vertical 9:16, photorealistic. Close-up: the man puts the wooden abacus down on the desk with a dramatic sigh, looks straight into the camera with comic despair and says in Uzbek: "Bu reklama-ku, yo lotereya?!" Warm light. Static camera.
Audio: abacus placed on wood, then silence.
Avoid: readable text, change of clothing.
```
> Klip 8 soniya chiqadi. Montajda faqat eng kuchli 3 soniyasini qoldiring.

### KLIP 5 · 31–36 s · DILNOZA BIRINCHI MARTA KO‘RINADI
Ingredients: Dilnoza + Dilnozaning xonasi
```
Vertical 9:16, photorealistic, cinematic. Start on a close-up of a smartphone lying on a wooden desk next to a blue-and-white Uzbek tea piala in the home office from the reference. A woman's hand picks up the phone, and the camera slowly tilts up to reveal the woman from the reference. She looks at the screen, sighs and says quietly in Uzbek, slightly disappointed: "Postni yubormadi-yu..." Then she glances sideways toward the camera with a slightly mysterious look. Cool soft daylight from the window, calmer and cooler color tone than the previous scenes. Slow tilt-up, then a gentle push-in on her face.
Audio: quiet room ambience, a soft mysterious musical sting at the end.
Avoid: readable text on the phone, logos, other people, pets, change of glasses or hairstyle.
```

### MONTAJ BLOKI · 36–39 s · CTA
- Ekranda: **"Narx so‘rab charchagan do‘stingizga yuboring 📩"**
- Pastda: *"Ertaga: uchinchi admin kim edi?"*

## Flow bo‘yicha maslahatlar
1. 1, 2, 3 va 4-kliplarni ketma-ket, bir xil Ingredients bilan yarating: Rustam akaning kurtkasi va soati hamma klipda bir xil chiqishi kerak.
2. Agar Rustam aka 1-kundagidan farq qilsa, 1-kundagi eng yaxshi klipning bir kadrini skrinshot qilib, **Frames to Video**'ga boshlang‘ich kadr qilib bering.
3. 5-klipda ranglar salqinroq bo‘lishi ataylab qilingan: tomoshabin "boshqa joy, boshqa odam" ekanini darhol sezadi.
4. Instagramga joylaganda **"AI info"** belgisini yoqing.
